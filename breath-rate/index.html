4 · How to test
#🧪 Test👀 What to look for
1Sit still, breathe through the mouth, count your own breaths for 60 s.Reading matches your count. If it reads about double, tick divide by 2.
2Watch Figure 2 while breathing slowly and deeply.Which frequencies light up with each breath. That is the real breath band on your AirPods.
3After 30 s of steady breathing, read Figure 4 and tap Use suggested band.Whether the suggested band differs from 150 Hz to 2 kHz, and whether the reading gets steadier.
4Talk for 30 s with the tag set to voice and record a clip.Where speech sits in Figure 2 compared with breath. That is what a rejection rule has to separate.
5Walk, then easy jog, recording breathing-walk and breathing-run.Whether footsteps (low, periodic) and wind (broad, flat) overlap the breath band.
6Save both CSVs after each session.Clips plus CSVs are the data for the next tuning pass.
5 · How it works
#⚙️ Stage📐 Detail
1CapturegetUserMedia with echo cancellation, noise suppression and auto gain turned off, so the breath hiss is not filtered away.
2Spectrum tapA second analyser on the raw signal, before the filter: 2048-point FFT every 25 ms, feeding Figures 2 to 4.
3Band-passSecond-order high-pass and low-pass, default 150 Hz and 2 kHz, adjustable live from Figure 3 or the suggested band.
4EnvelopeRMS energy every 25 ms in dB, smoothed with a 150 ms time constant, minus a 4 s baseline.
5DetectThreshold at k times the running standard deviation (8 s), with hysteresis at half the threshold and a minimum gap between events.
6Rate60 divided by the median interval between events in the last 30 s.
7Band score48 log-spaced bands from 60 Hz up. For each band: mean dB in the 0.45 s before each breath peak minus mean dB more than 0.8 s from any peak (the contrast), kept only where the contrast is at least one standard deviation. Suggested band: the highest-contrast band, widened while neighbours reach at least half its contrast.
6 · Known limits
#⚠️ Limit🔧 What to do
1The band score uses breaths detected through the current band, so it is biased toward that band.Read it with Figure 2. If breaths show clearly outside the band, move the cutoffs there by hand and let the score update.
2iOS may still apply its own processing to the AirPods mic, and the Bluetooth mic may be band-limited to about 8 kHz.Figure 3 shows it directly: a cliff in the spectrum marks the real bandwidth.
3Speech is loud and bursty in the same range, so it reads as fast breathing.The voice clips and Figure 2 are for building a rejection rule.
4Safari pauses the page when the screen locks.The page asks for a screen wake lock; keep it in the foreground while testing.
5Wind outdoors is broadband and can bury the signal.Compare indoor and outdoor clips before tuning anything.
