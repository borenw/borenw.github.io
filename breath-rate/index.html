3 · How to test
#🧪 Test👀 What to look for
1Sit still, breathe through the mouth, count your own breaths for 60 s.Reading matches your count. If it reads about double, tick divide by 2.
2Talk for 30 s with the tag set to voice and record a clip.How many false events speech makes. This is the main thing to reject later.
3Walk indoors, then outdoors, recording breathing-walk.Whether footsteps and wind swamp the trace.
4Easy jog with two-and-two breathing, recording breathing-run.The reading should sit near your step cadence divided by 4.
5Save the CSV after each session.Clips plus CSV are the data for tuning the filter and threshold.
4 · How it works
#⚙️ Stage📐 Detail
1CapturegetUserMedia with echo cancellation, noise suppression and auto gain turned off, so the breath hiss is not filtered away.
2Band-passHigh-pass 150 Hz, low-pass 2 kHz. Removes footstrike thump and most hiss above the breath band.
3EnvelopeRMS energy every 25 ms in dB, smoothed with a 150 ms time constant.
4DetrendSubtract a 4 s baseline so slow level changes do not move the threshold.
5DetectThreshold at k times the running standard deviation (8 s), with hysteresis at half the threshold and a minimum gap between events.
6Rate60 divided by the median interval between events in the last 30 s. The median ignores the odd missed or extra event.
5 · Known limits
#⚠️ Limit🔧 What to do
1iOS may still apply its own processing to the AirPods mic stream, which can weaken quiet nasal breathing.Test mouth breathing first. If the trace is flat, that is the answer to the feasibility question.
2Speech is loud and bursty in the same band, so it reads as fast breathing.The voice clips are for building a rejection rule in v0.2.
3Safari pauses the page when the screen locks.The page asks for a screen wake lock; keep it in the foreground while testing.
4Wind outdoors is broadband and can bury the signal.Compare indoor and outdoor clips before tuning anything.
