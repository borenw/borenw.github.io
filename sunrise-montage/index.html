2. 📥 Get a year of photos

The PhenoCam Network has 700+ fixed cameras, most shooting every 30 minutes (NEON sites every 15), many with years of continuous images. A free account lets you download in bulk.

#📋 Step💡 Detail
1Create a free PhenoCam accountNeeded to download images.
2Pick a scenic siteChoose one that keeps shooting before sunrise all year. Some only shoot in full daylight, which hides the wave.
3Download one full yearSet the date range to 12 months and the time window to about 04:00 to 09:00.
4Unzip and loadOn a computer, use Pick folder. On a phone, save the JPGs to the Files app and use Pick photos.
5GenerateSet latitude and longitude to the site and turn on the sunrise curve to check alignment.
⚠️ Dates and times are read from file names such as site_2025_06_01_053006.jpg, IMG_20250601_053006.jpg or 2025-06-01 05-30.jpg. If a name has no date, the file's modified time is used, which is often wrong for downloaded files.

3. 🔬 How it works

🧩 Part📐 What it does
➡️ ColumnsDays of the year, left to right, one per day or per chosen step.
⬇️ RowsTime of morning, top to bottom, one per time slot.
🌗 The edgeCells above sunrise are dark, cells below are lit. The boundary is sunrise time, which swings early in summer and late in winter.
〰️ Why a sineSun declination follows the year almost like a sine (period 365 days), so sunrise time does too, plus a small wobble from the equation of time.
📏 AmplitudeGrows with latitude. Near the equator the wave is nearly flat; far north it is tall.

4. 🛠️ Tips and troubleshooting

❓ Issue✅ Fix
Black cellsNo photo for that slot. Missing photos look like night, so pick a site with few gaps, or use 1 week columns to bridge them.
Staircase lookUse 10 or 15 minute rows and 1 day columns for a smoother wave.
A jump in March and NovemberThe camera clock follows daylight saving time. The jump is real data, not a bug.
Slow on a phoneEvery photo is decoded in the browser. Use 1 week columns or a computer for a full daily year.
Photos picked from the iPhone Photos app have wrong datesThe picker can rename files. Save them to the Files app first and pick from there.
