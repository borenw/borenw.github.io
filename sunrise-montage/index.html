2. 📥 Get a real year of photos

The PhenoCam Network keeps years of photos from 700+ fixed cameras. Most shoot every 30 minutes; NEON tower cameras shoot every 15. The script below downloads one year of morning photos from one site. It needs only Python 3 (built into macOS and Linux), nothing to install.

📋 Copy-ready download command

Paste into Terminal (Mac or Linux). Installs the script, prints its version, then downloads NEON GRSM for 2025.
mkdir -p ~/bin && curl -fsSL https://borenw.github.io/sunrise-montage/phenocam_sunrise.py -o ~/bin/phenocam_sunrise.py && chmod +x ~/bin/phenocam_sunrise.py && python3 ~/bin/phenocam_sunrise.py -V && python3 ~/bin/phenocam_sunrise.py NEON.D07.GRSM.DP1.00033 2025
Run again later (resumes where it stopped, skips photos you already have):
python3 ~/bin/phenocam_sunrise.py NEON.D07.GRSM.DP1.00033 2025
#📋 Step💡 Detail
1Paste the commandPhotos land in phenocam_<SITE>_<YEAR>/ in the folder you ran it from. A status line every 5 seconds shows progress, rate and time left.
2WaitA full year at 15 minutes from 04:00 to 09:00 is about 7,000 photos and roughly 1 to 3 GB.
3LoadChoose the matching NEON preset above, then Pick folder (computer) or Pick photos (phone, from the Files app).
4GenerateRows switch to 15 minutes to match the camera. Turn on the sunrise curve to check that the edge lines up.

3. 🔬 How it works

🧩 Part📐 What it does
➡️ ColumnsDays of the year, left to right.
⬇️ RowsTime of morning, top to bottom.
🌗 The edgeCells above sunrise are dark, cells below are lit. The boundary is sunrise time, early in summer and late in winter.
〰️ Why a sineThe sun's declination follows the year almost like a sine (period 365 days), so sunrise time does too, plus a small wobble from the equation of time.
📏 AmplitudeGrows with latitude: nearly flat in the Everglades, enormous at Denali.
🍂 SeasonsThe demo also browns the trees in winter and adds snow at Denali and Rocky Mountain, like a real camera would see.

4. 🛠️ Tips and troubleshooting

❓ Issue✅ Fix
Black cellsNo photo for that slot. Keep Fill gaps on; it copies the nearest photo in time (within 30 minutes) or from a neighbouring day.
Rows finer than the cameraAt 7.5 minutes with a 15 minute camera, every other row is a copy. Use 15 minute rows for real NEON data.
A jump in March and NovemberThat camera follows daylight saving time. PhenoCam uses standard time, so its data should not jump.
Slow on a phoneEvery photo is decoded in the browser. Use 1 week columns on a phone, or a computer for a full daily year.
Photos from the iPhone Photos app have wrong datesThe picker can rename files. Save them to the Files app first and pick from there.
